Copy link puts the whole setup — answers, clues, colors, fonts and poster size — into a link you can send to yourself or a co-conspirator. Opening it restores the input and generates straight away. The address bar keeps up as you type, so a refresh never loses your work.

A clue ending in ? is kept as a question. Anything else is treated as a sentence, and the answer is blanked out for you wherever it appears — or write your own ___. Lines without a clue may list several words separated by commas. Answers can be numbers too: 2019: The year we met.

The same answer list always produces the same layouts — regenerating, reloading, or opening a shared link anywhere shows identical results. Asking for more results searches a little longer; word lists with few shared letters may simply not have that many distinct layouts.
